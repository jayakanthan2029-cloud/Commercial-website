Common Questions

Everything you need to know about getting your business online.

How fast can you create my website?
+
We deliver most websites within 48 business hours. After you provide your business details and approve the demo, we configure and deploy your live website quickly. Rush delivery options are available for urgent cases.
Do I need to pay upfront?
+
No! We follow a "Pay After Preview" model. You get a free custom demo of your website first. Only after you approve it, you make the payment. We want you to be 100% satisfied before paying.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
+
Absolutely! All our websites are 100% mobile-responsive. Your customers will have a perfect experience on phones, tablets, and computers. We test on all devices before delivery.
Can I update my website content myself?
+
Yes! We provide you with an easy-to-use admin dashboard where you can update products, prices, services, and more. No technical knowledge needed. We also offer free support for the first 3 months.
What's included in the WhatsApp integration?
+
Customers can click the WhatsApp button on your website and start a conversation with you directly. Orders, inquiries, and booking requests come straight to your WhatsApp inbox. It's the easiest way to stay connected with your customers.
